## About
Camp Loop is a web app built at Firebytes for CodinginColor to boost student engagement and build lasting community connections. Many programs struggle with low student survey responses and lose touch with students after they graduate. Our platform solves both by rewarding students for completing surveys and connecting high school alumni back with current students for weekly talks and volunteer hour tutoring.


## Features
**Passwordless Email Sign-In:** Students register and log in with just a simple email address, making it easy to stay connected even after finishing a course.
- **Weekly Check-In Surveys with Unique Codes:** Students receive weekly emails upon completion of a survey (once a week) , helping Coding in Color track long-term skill retention and gather ongoing feedback.
- **Points & Rewards System:** Students earn points for every survey they complete and can redeem their balance for prizes on the Rewards page.
- **Alumni Mentorship Q&A Board:** Current students can post coding questions and get help on assignments directly from Coding in Color alumni.
- **Impact & Feedback Dashboard:** Gives Coding in Color clear visibility into student engagement, post course progress, and program feedback over time.
